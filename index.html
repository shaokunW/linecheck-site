A keyboard-controlled reading guide for Chrome. Follow the text, mark what you have reviewed, and keep your place.

Preparing for the Chrome Web Store

Read at a pace that works for you.

Keep the words in focus. Let everything else fade.

Use ↑ ↓ to move, Enter to mark, and F to flag.

Read with the keyboard

Move between lines with ↑ ↓. Magnify the current line with ← → without changing the page layout. Controls sit below the reading line.

Mark with intention

Green for reviewed text, red for a second look. Repeat the same action to remove a mark.

Keep records local

Your page records stay in local browser storage. No LineCheck account or developer upload required.

Built for everyday text

English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어. Works on ordinary text-based webpages. PDF viewers, browser pages, embedded frames, and some complex layouts are not supported. LineCheck assists manual review; it does not automatically correct spelling or grammar.
